3. The "Iceberg" TCO Model

LCC Engineering Formula

$$ LCC = C_{acq} + \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{C_{maint} + C_{fail}}{(1+r)^t} + \mathbf{C_{eff}} $$
C_acq Acquisition Cost (Initial CAPEX)
C_fail Revenue lost during forced outages
C_eff Revenue lost due to Efficiency Droop
