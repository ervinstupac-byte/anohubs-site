Precision Shaft
Alignment Protocol

Dynamic instability accounts for 48% of premature failures. We do not accept "good enough." This protocol defines the rigid standard for turbine-generator alignment, including plumbness and thrust bearing equalization.

CRITICAL TOLERANCE

Total shaft runout must not exceed 0.05 mm per meter (0.002 in/ft). Exceeding this limit instantly voids the Engineering Immunity guarantee.

Pre-Alignment Prerequisites

Soft Foot

STEP 1

Loosen each bolt. If movement > 0.03mm is detected, the foundation is compromised.

Thermal Growth

STEP 2

Calculate operational expansion (ΔT). Set targets to offset cold alignment.

Pipe Strain

STEP 3

Ensure no external forces from cooling pipes distort the machine casing.
