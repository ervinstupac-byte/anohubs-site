History & Evolution ↓

01 // THE LEGACY

From 1848 to CFD Perfection

Developed by James B. Francis in Lowell, Massachusetts, this design revolutionized hydropower by combining radial and axial flow concepts. Before Francis, turbines were inefficient "pinwheels". His analytical approach to vane curvature created the first scientifically optimized hydraulic machine.

Today's Reality: Modern Francis units achieve efficiencies exceeding 96%. However, this peak performance narrows the operating band. Pushing a modern Francis into deep part-load (<40%) creates violent Inter-Blade Vortexes.

03 // FAILURE MODES

The "Invisible" Threats Inside the Spiral Case

01

Rotor-Stator Interaction (RSI)

This is the pressure pulsation that occurs between the passing runner blades and the stationary guide vanes. It creates a high-frequency hydraulic hammer. If this frequency aligns with the natural frequency of the runner, you get catastrophic resonance.

PROTOCOL: Frequency Analysis & Von Karman Check
02

Draft Tube Vortex Rope

At part load operation, the residual swirl leaving the runner creates a corkscrew vortex in the draft tube. This causes massive pressure surges ("surging") that can lift the entire generator rotor off its bearings.

PROTOCOL: Air Injection & CFD Mapping
