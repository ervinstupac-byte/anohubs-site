01 // THE LEGACY

Lester Pelton's Breakthrough

In the California Gold Rush, inefficient water wheels wasted energy. Lester Pelton invented the split-bucket design, which reverses the water jet nearly 180°, extracting almost every joule of kinetic energy.

Today's Reality: Modern Peltons operate at heads over 1000m. The jet velocity exceeds 140 m/s. At this speed, a misalignment of 0.5mm destroys the bucket surface in weeks.

03 // FAILURE MODES

Impulse Failure Mechanisms

01

Sand/Silt Abrasion

High-velocity water turns glacial silt into liquid sandpaper. It erodes the splitter and bucket cut-out, destroying the hydraulic profile and reducing efficiency by 3-5% in a single season.

PROTOCOL: HVOF Coating & Profile Mapping
02

Jet Misalignment

If the jet doesn't hit the splitter dead-center, energy is wasted and asymmetrical load destroys the runner bearings. A deviation of just 1 degree creates massive eccentric forces.

PROTOCOL: Laser Calibration (Dry Test)
