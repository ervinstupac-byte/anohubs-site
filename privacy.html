Privacy Protocol

Last Updated: December 2025

We do not collect personal data beyond direct communication forms. No cookies are used for advertising tracking. Data is processed solely for technical correspondence.

When you contact us through our inquiry forms, we collect only the information you explicitly provide: name, email, company, and message content. This data is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry.

We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking. Any technical cookies used are strictly functional (e.g., language preference) and stored locally in your browser.

Data Storage & Security

Communication data is processed through secure form handlers and stored only as long as necessary for technical correspondence. We do not sell, share, or distribute your information to third parties.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us through our standard inquiry form or email listed on the contact page.
