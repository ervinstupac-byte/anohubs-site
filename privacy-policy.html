DATA SECURITY
PROTOCOL

How we protect your operational intelligence within the AnoHUB ecosystem. Last updated: NOV 2025.

This Privacy Protocol describes how Anohubs ("we", "us", or "our") manages data integrity. Just as we demand zero tolerance for turbine failure, we demand zero compromise on data security.

1. Intelligence Collection

1.1 Operational Data

When you initiate a consultation, we collect specific technical parameters (Name, Coordinates, Plant Specifications) solely for the purpose of audit planning.

1.2 Telemetry (Cookies)

We use essential technical cookies for site functionality. We do not use invasive trackers. Your operational footprint remains private.

2. Data Utilization Logic

  • Execution of Engineering Immunity protocols and audits.
  • Optimization of our AI prediction models (using anonymized datasets).
  • Establishing secure communication channels for project management.

3. Defense Mechanisms

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards (ISO 27001 aligned) to protect your information from loss, theft, and unauthorized access.

> NO THIRD-PARTY DATA SALES PERMITTED.
> EXTERNAL SHARING RESTRICTED TO SECURE PARTNERS.

4. Exercise Your Rights

If you wish to request a data purge or review your stored information, contact the Data Governance Officer directly.
