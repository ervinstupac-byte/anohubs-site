Beyond the Kilowatt-Hour: A Call for a New Symbiosis Standard

Why we must give economic weight to ecological health, biodiversity, and natural capital beyond mere energy output.

For years, I’ve worked on the ground, in the heart of hydropower. But it wasn't until a recent visit to the Una river in Bosnia and Herzegovina that I truly saw the hidden value we so often miss.

The Revelation of the River Mint

I’ve been to the breathtaking Štrbački buk waterfall many times. It was only on a recent trip that I realized the unique scent I loved came from river mint. We have become experts at measuring the obvious—the kilowatt-hour. But what about the river mint?

The Problem with Our Current Model

The reality is that our current approach is incomplete. We treat hydropower as a pure financial equation, ignoring the ecological and natural capital.

Visionary Insight: We need a quantifiable counter-value system where the preservation of nature is given real economic weight.

A Vision for the Future: The Symbiosis Standard

I believe we need a new, international standard for project viability that looks beyond mere energy output. I call this the Symbiosis Standard.
