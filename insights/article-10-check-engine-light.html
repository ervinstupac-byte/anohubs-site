The 'Check Engine Light' of Your Turbine: 5 Symptoms You Should Never Ignore

A practical guide to the 5 subtle symptoms (noise, vibration, temperature, leaks) that signal impending failure in hydropower turbines.

Imagine this: a perfectly sunny day, everything is running smoothly, and then, without warning, an alarm sounds. Your hydropower plant shuts down unexpectedly. But turbines, just like cars, often send warning signals before they fail.

Understanding the Hidden Symptoms

Unexpected failures don't happen overnight. They are the result of cumulative stress from issues like abrasion, cavitation, and bearing wear.

Your Diagnostic Toolkit: 5 Key Indicators

  1. Unusual Noise

    Thumping indicates cavitation. Squealing signals bearing issues.

  2. Changes in Vibration

    Vibrations intensify over time and can signal an unbalanced rotor.

  3. Temperature Spikes

    High temperatures can indicate insufficient lubrication or excessive friction.

  4. Water Leaks

    Foamy water or oily streaks are signs of seal failure.

  5. Power Output Drops

    Unexplained drops in output signal wear or blockage.

Don't Ignore the Signs
