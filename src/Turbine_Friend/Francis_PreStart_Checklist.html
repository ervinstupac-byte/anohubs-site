PRE-START VERIFICATION CHECKLIST
FRANCIS HORIZONTAL TURBINE - UNIT 1
Mechanical Verification
Safety Pins Removed: Verify all shipping/maintenance safety pins have been removed from coupling, governor linkage, and brake mechanisms. Visual confirmation required.
Coupling Bolts Torqued: All coupling bolts torqued to specification (refer to coupling SOP) with Loctite 243 applied. Torque values recorded in logbook.
Shaft Alignment Verified: Gaps measured at 12, 3, 6, 9 o'clock positions. Maximum deviation < 0.05mm. Values logged in precision logbook.
Governor Linkage Free Movement: Guide vane linkage operates smoothly through full range (0-100%). No binding or excessive friction detected.
Bearing Housings Secured: All bearing housing bolts tight. No visible oil leaks from seals or gaskets.
Fluid & Hydraulic Verification
HPU Pressure at Nominal: Hydraulic Power Unit operating at 150-160 bar. Accumulator pre-charge verified. No pump cavitation noise.
Nitrogen Pre-Charge Confirmed: Accumulator nitrogen pre-charge at 120 bar (±5 bar). Date of last charge within 6 months.
Cooling Water Valves Open: Inlet and outlet valves fully open. Visual confirmation of water flow through sight glass. Temperature differential < 10°C.
Bearing Oil Level Verified: Main bearing and guide bearing oil levels within sight glass limits. Oil quality visually acceptable (clear, no emulsification).
Sump Pumps Operational: Powerhouse sump pumps tested and functioning. High-water alarm tested and operational.
Electrical & Control Verification
Brushes Properly Seated: Carbon brushes inspected and properly seated on slip rings. Spring tension verified. No excessive dust accumulation.
Megger Test Passed: Insulation resistance test performed on stator windings. Resistance > 100 MΩ at 500V DC. Results logged.
SCADA Communication Green: All sensors reporting to SCADA. No communication errors. RTD, pressure transducers, and vibration sensors online.
Emergency Stop Tested: E-stop button functionality verified. System responds with immediate shutdown command.
Protection Relays Set: Overcurrent, differential, and ground fault protection relays verified at correct settings per manufacturer specifications.
READY FOR START-UP
