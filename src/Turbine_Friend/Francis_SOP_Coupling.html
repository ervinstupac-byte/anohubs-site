Critical SOP: Coupling & Shaft Alignment

Ref: 1.3 MW Units | The "Zero Stress" Objective

⚠️ THE FOUNDATION SHIFT RISK

Context: Since 2014, the powerhouse foundation has likely settled. For a horizontal unit spinning at 750/1000 RPM, the margin for error is microscopic.

  • The Threat: A vertical foundation shift of just 0.1mm creates a permanent bending force ("Cyclic Loading") on the shaft.
  • Result: Fatigue failure of the shaft or catastrophic bearing seizure.
SOP 1: Alignment Logic

We are fighting two types of misalignment. Both must be eliminated.

  • 1. Parallel (Offset): The generator shaft axis is higher/lower or left/right of the turbine axis.
  • 2. Angular (Gap): The faces of the coupling are not parallel (open at the top or side).
Goal: When the bolts are tight, the shaft should naturally "float" in the bearings, not be forced there.
SOP 2: Precision Measurement (Rim & Face)

Method: Use a Dial Indicator with a magnetic base clamped to one shaft hub, measuring the other.

2.1 The Procedure

  1. Setup: Clean the coupling flanges. Mount the dial indicator to read the Rim (Radial/Parallel) and the Face (Axial/Angular).
  2. Rotate: Rotate both shafts together (if coupled) or the generator shaft (if uncoupled) in 90° increments (12:00, 3:00, 6:00, 9:00).
  3. Calculate: Record the Total Indicated Reading (TIR). Halve the TIR to get the actual offset.

2.2 The Target Tolerance

  • Pass: Misalignment ≤ 0.05 mm.
  • Fail: Misalignment > 0.05 mm. Shims must be added/removed from generator feet.
SOP 3: Coupling Bolt Integrity

A loose coupling bolt destroys the flange holes ("ovaling"), rendering the shaft scrap.

3.1 Inspection & Torque

  • Rubber Elements: If flexible pin coupling, inspect rubber bushes for cracking/dry rot. Replace as a full set.
  • Rigid Bolts: Inspect fit. Bolts must be a "Transition Fit" (Zero play).
  • Chemical Locking:
    • Vibration is constant. Mechanical washers are insufficient.
    • Standard: Apply Loctite 243 (Blue) to threads during final torque sequence.
SOP 4: Symptom Awareness

How do you know if alignment has drifted before the shaft breaks?

4.1 The "Coupling Hum"

  • Sound: A distinct, low-frequency "Growl" or "Hum" coming specifically from the coupling guard area.
  • Vibration: High 1X RPM vibration signature in the Radial direction.

4.2 The "Hot Bearing" Sign

  • Symptom: The bearing closest to the coupling runs 5-10°C hotter than the outboard bearing.
  • Cause: The coupling is physically lifting or pushing down on that specific bearing, breaking the oil film.
Summary Checklist
  • Every 6 Months: Check Rim & Face Alignment.
  • Annually: Replace Rubber Coupling Elements (if flexible).
  • Consumable: Loctite 243 (Blue) always in stock.
