Critical SOP: Unit 1 Recovery

Target: Shaft Seal Replacement & Distributor Sync

⚠️ MANDATORY SAFETY PROTOCOL

STOP. READ THIS FIRST.

  • Mechanical Isolation: Before touching the shaft or distributor, the Mechanical Safety Pin MUST be inserted into the Main Inlet Valve (MIV).
  • Zero Energy: Do not rely principally on hydraulic pressure, SCADA signals, or "Closed" indicators. Only the physical steel pin guarantees life safety.
  • Clearance: Use the "Clean-First" rule. Remove all silt and mud from the turbine pit to expose bolts and monitoring lines.
SOP 1: Shaft Seal Replacement (Unit 1 Focus)

Unit 1 is currently stopped due to seal failure caused by silt ingestion. The following procedure is required to restore integrity.

Step 1: Access & Inspection

  1. Disconnect leakage monitoring pipes carefully to allow seal housing movement.
  2. Remove the housing cover bolts in a star pattern.
  3. CRITICAL INSPECTION: Once the sleeve is exposed, check for "Scouring" (deep vertical scratches caused by silt).
💡 Tech Note: If the sleeve has deep scratches, new packing will not seal. The sleeve must be polished (fine grit sandpaper, maintain diameter) or replaced if damage > 0.5mm.

Step 2: Staggered Installation (The 90° Rule)

The leading cause of repeat failure is aligned cuts.

  • Insert packing rings one by one.
  • Stagger the cuts: Ensure the joint of Ring 1 is at 12:00, Ring 2 is at 3:00 (or 4:00), etc.
  • Offset: Maintain a 90° or 120° offset between layers. NEVER align cuts.
[Visualization: Ring 1 (12:00) ➔ Ring 2 (3:00) ➔ Ring 3 (6:00) ➔ Ring 4 (9:00)]

Step 3: Closing & Priming

  1. Re-install housing cover. Tighten gland follower evenly to avoid jamming.
  2. Prime the Seal: Manually trigger the central greasing system to fill the new packing with grease before the shaft rotates.
SOP 2: Distributor Synchronization

Efficiency loss is often due to "drifting" guide vanes. We verify synchronization mechanically, then electronically.

Step 1: Fabricate the "L-Metal" Tool

  • Create a custom metal profile (Shape "L").
  • Dimensions: Must fit exactly between the edge of a guide vane and the adjacent fixed casing ("Chin gap") when the vane is in the fully CLOSED position.
  • This tool becomes the site standard for "Zero Position".

Step 2: Mechanical Verification (The 20-Point Check)

With the distributor mechanically locked in the CLOSED position:

  1. Walk to Vane #1. Insert the L-Metal tool.
  2. Repeat for all 20 Guide Vanes.
  3. Pass Criteria: Tool fits snugly.
  4. Fail Criteria: Tool is loose (Vane not closed) OR Tool doesn't fit (Vane over-closed).

Step 3: SCADA Cross-Reference

Once all 20 vanes are mechanically verified as closed:

  • Check the SCADA Position Sensor.
  • Value must read 0.0%.
  • If > 0.5%, re-calibrate the linear transducer at the servomotor.
Materials & Tools Checklist
  • Replacement Seal: Carbon/Teflon Packing Rings.
  • Chemicals: Loctite 243 (Blue) for housing bolts.
  • Tools: Fine grit sandpaper (Sleeve polishing), L-Metal Gauge.
  • Safety: MIV Safety Pin.
