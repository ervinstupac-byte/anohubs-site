CRITICAL FLOODING RISK

If leakage exceeds 10 L/min, ignore this SOP and initiate EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN (ESD). Do not attempt manual tightening while rotating > 100 RPM.

01 DRAINAGE ACTIVATION

Ensure turbine pit pumps are in AUTO/ON mode. Verify discharge flow.

> CHECK PUMP_1 STATUS [ON]
> CHECK PUMP_2 STATUS [STANDBY]

02 SEAL WATER PRESSURE

Increase clean water supply pressure to the carbon ring assembly. Target: 0.5 Bar > Penstock Pressure.

03 INFLATABLE MAINTENANCE SEAL

ONLY if unit is STOPPED (0 RPM): Inflate the maintenance seal to isolate the shaft.
