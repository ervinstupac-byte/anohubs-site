Critical SOP: Intake & Silt Management

Maximizing Head & Minimizing Erosion

⚠️ THE REVENUE KILLER: DEBRIS

Logic: Every leaf, branch, or plastic bag on the trash rack creates a restriction.

  • Head Loss ($\Delta H$): Head Loss = Lost Power. A 1-meter drop in head due to a blocked rack is pure financial waste.
  • The Clean-First Rule: A clean intake is the first line of defense. If debris passes the rack, it destroys MIV seals and blocks guide vanes.
SOP 1: Trash Rack Protocols

The trash rack is the lungs of the hydro system. It must breathe freely.

1.1 Cleaning Trigger

  • Monitoring: Watch the differential pressure sensor (or water level difference) between the river and the intake canal.
  • Action Limit: Initiate cleaning when differential exceeds 20 cm (0.2m).
  • Method: Use the automatic rake or manual cleaning. Ensure debris is removed from the platform, not pushed back into the water.
SOP 2: Silt Basin (Sand Trap) Management

Our rivers are silt-heavy. The Sand Trap is designed to settle out abrasive particles before they reach the turbine.

2.1 The Synergistic Threat

  • Mechanism: If the basin fills up, coarse sand enters the penstock.
  • Damage: Sand acts as an abrasive, stripping the oxide layer off the runner/guide vanes. This accelerates Cavitation by providing a rough surface for bubbles to form (Synergy).

2.2 Flushing Procedure

  1. Inspection: Check silt levels weekly (or daily during monsoon).
  2. Execute: Open the purge/flush gates to wash accumulated sediment back to the river.
  3. Verify: Ensure the flushing tunnel is clear and flow returns to the turbine only after the basin water is visibly clearer.
SOP 3: Downstream Protection

What happens at the intake decides the fate of the machine hall.

  • MIV Seals: Large debris that bypasses a broken trash rack will lodge in the MIV seal, preventing closure.
  • Guide Vanes: Sticks and stones can jam guide vanes, causing shear pin failure and emergency shutdowns.
Rule: "If you see it floating at the intake, you will hear it grinding in the turbine."
Summary Checklist
  • Daily: Check Trash Rack differential. Clean if > 20cm.
  • Weekly: Flush Silt Basin (Sand Trap).
  • Post-Storm: Immediate inspection of Trash Rack for heavy debris load.
