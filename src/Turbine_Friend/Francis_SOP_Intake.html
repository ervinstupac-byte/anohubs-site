01 ⚠️ REVENUE KILLER: TRASH & DEBRIS

Logic: Every leaf, branch, or plastic bag on the rack creates flow resistance.

  • Head Loss ($\Delta H$): Head Loss = Lost Power. A 1-meter drop due to a blocked rack is pure financial loss.
  • Cleanliness Rule: A clean intake is the first line of defense. If trash passes the rack, it destroys MIV seals and blocks guide vanes.

02 CLEANING TRIGGER

The intake rack is the lungs of the hydro system. It must breathe freely.

  • Monitoring: Watch the differential pressure sensor (or water level difference) between river and intake channel.
  • Action Limit: Initiate cleaning when difference exceeds 20 cm (0.2m).
  • Method: Use automatic cleaner or manual rake. Ensure debris is removed from platform, not pushed back into water.

03 SEDIMENT THREAT & FLUSHING

If the settling basin fills, coarse sand enters the penstock acting as an abrasive.

Synergistic Threat

  • Mechanism: Sand strips the oxide layer off the runner/guide vanes.
  • Damage: This accelerates Cavitation by providing a rough surface for bubble formation.

Flushing Procedure

  1. Inspection: Check silt level weekly (daily during high water).
  2. Execution: Open bottom outlets to flush accumulated sediment back to river.
  3. Verification: Ensure flushing tunnel is clear and flow returns to turbine only after basin water is visually clearer.

OPERATOR CHECKLIST

  • Daily: Check Rack Differential. Clean if > 20cm.
  • Weekly: Flush Settling Basin (Sand Trap).
  • Post-Storm: Emergency Rack Inspect due to heavy debris.
