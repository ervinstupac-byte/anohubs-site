Pressure Equalization & Physical Sealing

Preventing wire-drawing erosion and ensuring reliable sequencing

⚠️ CRITICAL OPERATING RULE

NEVER FORCE OPEN THE MIV.

  • Differential Pressure Limit: The MIV cylinder is NOT designed to open against full penstock pressure. ΔP must be < 2 Bar.
  • Seal Damage: Opening against differential pressure rips the main seal from its seat.
  • Safety: Hydraulic overload can burst cylinder seals or hoses.
The 0% Close Rule - Zero Tolerance

SCADA vs. Physical Reality

The Trap: SCADA showing "0% Open" does not guarantee a seal. A limit switch can be bumped or misaligned.

The Law of Erosion (Wire-Drawing):

  • If the MIV is physically open by even 2° (e.g., at 88° instead of 90° seat angle), a high-velocity jet of water passes through the gap.
  • Velocity: > 40 m/s under 150m head.
  • Effect: Cuts through stainless steel seat rings like a laser cutter within weeks.

Verification Procedure:

  1. Visual Check: Look at the mechanical counterweight arm position. Mark the true "Closed" position on the wall/floor.
  2. Leakage Check: With MIV closed and Bypass closed, open the Spiral Case Drain.
    • Flow should stop completely within 5 minutes.
    • If water continues to flow > 5 L/min, the MIV seal is passing.
Bypass Interlock Logic - The Pressure Handshake

Opening Sequence Authorization

The PLC MUST enforce the following logic chain before energizing the MIV opening solenoid:

Step Action Condition to Proceed
1 Command "Start Unit" No Shutdown Faults
2 Open Bypass Valve Bypass Limit Switch = OPEN
3 Wait for Spiral Case Fill Pressure Rise > 2 Bar/sec
4 Pressure Equalization Check (P_penstock - P_spiral) < 0.5 Bar
5 Command MIV Open Equalization CONFIRMED

The "Leaking Distributor" Symptom

Scenario: Bypass valve opens, but Spiral Case pressure never equalizes with Penstock pressure.

Diagnosis: The Guide Vanes (Distributor) are leaking water into the runner faster than the Bypass can fill the spiral case.

Action:

  1. DO NOT override the interlock.
  2. Inspect Guide Vane seals mechanism.
  3. See Distributor Sync SOP.

Cylinder & Weld Audit Checklist

Mechanical Integrity Inspection

The MIV body endures the highest static stress of any component (16 Bar continuous).

Annual Inspection Points:

  1. Hydraulic Cylinder Mounting:
    • Check torque on trunnion mounting bolts.
    • Inspect pin bushings for ovality/slop (> 0.5mm play = replace).
  2. Weld Seams (NDT Required):
    • Perform Dye Penetrant test on flange-to-body welds.
    • Critical Zone: The transition from the valve body to the trunnion mount (highest stress riser).
  3. Counterweight Arm:
    • Inspect safety guard welds.
    • Ensure weight stack is secure and cannot shift.
