MIV & Distributor Integrity Protocol

Ref Code: MIV-DIST-01 | Revision: 2.0

CRITICAL INTERLOCK: This module is directly tied to the Mission Control Startup Sequence. Any failure here prevents unit start.

1. MIV Angular Calibration

The 0% Mechanical Zero

Requirement: The MIV seal ring must contact the seat evenly. A SCADA reading of 0% implies the limit switch is engaged, but not necessarily that the valve is water-tight.

Verification: Perform Spiral Case drain test. Leakage must be < 5 L/min.

The 80° Open Position

Requirement: The valve disc must align perfectly with the flow to minimize hydraulic losses.

Tolerance: 80° ± 0.5°. Any deviation creates a vortex shedder.

2. Distributor Synchronicity Check

The 20 Guide Vanes must move in unison. A single lagging vane creates a "jet" effect.

1
Lock Out Unit: De-energize HPU Oil Pressure.
2
Feeler Gauge Test: With distributor at 0% (Squeeze), verify 0.05mm feeler gauge cannot pass between ANY vane vertical seal.
3
Linkage Measurement: Measure distance from Regulating Ring pin to Vane lever arm. All 20 must match within 1.0mm.
Warning (Turbulence): If Vane #5 is open 2° more than others, it directs a high-velocity jet at the runner blade leading edge, causing cavitation pitting within 500 hours.
