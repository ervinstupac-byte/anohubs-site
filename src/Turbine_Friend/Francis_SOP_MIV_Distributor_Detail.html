2. DISTRIBUTOR SYNCHRONICITY

The 20 Guide Vanes must move in unison. A single lagging vane creates a "jet" effect that can destroy runner blades.

  • 01
    Lock Out Unit: De-energize HPU Oil Pressure totally.
  • 02
    Feeler Gauge Test: At 0% (Squeeze), verify 0.05mm gauge cannot pass between ANY vane seal.
  • 03
    Linkage Measurement: Measure Regulating Ring pin to Vane lever arm. All 20 must match ±1.0mm.
CAVITATION RISK: If Vane #5 is open 2° more than others, it directs a high-velocity jet at the runner, causing pitting within 500 hours.
