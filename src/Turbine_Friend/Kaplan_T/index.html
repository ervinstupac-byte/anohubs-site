About Kaplan Turbines

Kaplan turbines are axial flow reaction turbines with adjustable runner blades, designed for low head applications (2-40 meters). The unique double regulation system allows both guide vanes and runner blades to adjust, providing excellent efficiency across a wide range of flow conditions.

Note: This knowledge base is currently under development. Comprehensive SOPs for Kaplan turbines including blade pitch control, double regulation systems, and axial thrust management will be added soon.
