Critical SOP: Draft Tube & Tailrace

Energy Recovery & Vibration Control

⚠️ THE HIDDEN ENERGY THEFT

Logic: The Draft Tube (DFT) recovers 30-40% of the turbine's total power by decelerating the water and creating suction.

  • The Risk: If the draft tube elbow is silted, or the tailrace is blocked, the "Suction Head" is lost.
  • Impact: Every centimeter of "Backpressure" in the tailrace is a direct subtraction from the Net Head ($H_{net}$).
SOP 1: Elbow Inspection Strategy

In horizontal units, the 90° elbow is a sediment trap. Flow velocity drops here, allowing sand to settle.

1.1 Silt Accumulation Check

  1. Access: Enter via the draft tube manhole (Confined Space Permit required).
  2. Inspect: Check the bottom of the elbow for sand bars or rock piles.
  3. Clear: Remove all sediment. Turbulence caused by these piles destroys the draft tube efficiency.

1.2 Liner Integrity

  • Focus Area: Inspect the upper 1 meter of the draft tube liner (closest to runner).
  • Look For: "Pitting" or sponge-like metal texture (Cavitation).
  • Repair: If depth > 3mm, fill with Underwater Grade Concrete Repair Mortar or weld overlay.
SOP 2: Tailrace Exit & Debris

The water must leave as freely as it enters.

2.1 Obstruction Patrol

  • Vegetation: Overgrown bushes/trees in the channel increase friction. Clear back 2 meters from bank.
  • Rockfalls: Use a grappling hook or excessive flow (flushing) to clear fallen rocks from the tunnel exit.
  • Tailwater Level: If the level is higher than historical norms for the same flow, there is a downstream blockage.
SOP 3: Vibration Monitoring (ISO 10816)

The draft tube is a giant resonance chamber. It "sings" when unhappy.

3.1 Surging Detection

  • Symptom: Power swings (MW hunting) combined with a "chugging" sound.
  • Action: Adjust air admission (See Air Admission SOP) and check tailrace for "burping" air bubbles.
  • Limit: Structural vibration on the draft tube door should not exceed 2.5 mm/s RMS.
Rule: If the door bolts are shaking loose, the vortex is unstable. Admit more air.
Summary Checklist
  • Weekly: Walk the tailrace channe l to check for blockages.
  • Annual: Internal Confined Space inspection of DFT Elbow.
  • On Startup: Listen for "Rock Crushing" sound (Cavitation).
