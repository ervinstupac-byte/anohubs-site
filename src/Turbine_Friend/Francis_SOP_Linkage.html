Critical SOP: Governor & Guide Vane Linkage

Mechanical Synchronization & Transmission

⚠️ THE COST OF BACKLASH

Context: The hydraulic piston (Servomotor) pushes the Regulating Ring, which rotates all 20 guide vanes simultaneously to control water flow.

  • The Risk: If the linkage pins have "play" (wear), the vanes will flutter.
  • Equation: 1mm of pin wear = ~5% loss in vane synchronization. This leads to efficiency loss and hunting.
SOP 1: Linkage Logic & Inspection

The system is a chain. A chain is only as strong as its weakest link (pin).

1.1 Transmission Path

Servomotor → Regulating Ring → Linkage Arms (20x) → Guide Vane Stems.

1.2 Bushing & Pin Check

  1. Static Check: With the unit stopped and pressure released, grab each linkage arm by hand.
  2. Push/Pull: Attempt to wiggle the arm.
  3. Result:
    • Pass: Zero palpable movement. Solid.
    • Fail: "Clicking" sound or visible movement. Replace bushing immediately.
SOP 2: Lubrication Audit

Friction causes the governor to "hunt" (unstable power). Grease is the stabilizer.

2.1 Central Greasing System

  • Audit: Verify the central pump is building pressure (typically 150-200 bar).
  • Visual Confirmation: Inspect all 20 guide vane stems. You should see a fresh collar of grease purging from the seals.
  • Blockage: If one bearing is dry while others purge, the dosage valve is blocked. Clear it manually.
SOP 3: The Friction "Swing Test"

This test confirms the entire mechanism "floats" freely.

3.1 Procedure

  1. Safety: Unit stopped. MIV Closed. Mechanical Safety Pin REMOVED (Control risk!).
  2. Disconnect: Uncouple the servomotor piston from the regulating ring.
  3. Manual Swing: Two technicians should be able to push the regulating ring by hand from 0% to 100% open.
  4. Criteria: Movement must be smooth. No "binding", "hard spots", or "jumping".
Diagnosis: If the ring binds at a specific spot, a guide vane is bent or a debris rock is lodged in the casing.
Summary Checklist
  • Monthly: Check for "Grease Collars" on all 20 vanes.
  • Annually: Perform Manual "Swing Test" to check friction.
  • On Overhaul: Replace all shear pins and bushings if > 0.1mm play detected.
