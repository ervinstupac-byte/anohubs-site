Critical SOP: Generator Electrical & Brush Maintenance

Excitation, Insulation & Dust Control

⚠️ THE SILENT KILLER: CONDUCTIVE DUST

Context: Carbon brushes wear down over time, creating fine conductive dust.

  • The Danger: This dust settles on insulators and windings. In humid conditions, it creates a path for electricity to "flash over" to the ground.
  • Protocol: Cleanliness is not aesthetic; it is insulation.
SOP 1: Brush & Slip Ring Logic

The slip ring is the interface between the stationary excitation system and the rotating field.

1.1 Brush Inspection

  • Length: Replace brushes when they reach 25-30% of their original length. Do not wait for the pig-tail (wire) to touch the ring.
  • Spring Pressure: uneven pressure causes sparking.
    • Too soft = Sparking/Burning.
    • Too hard = Rapid mechanical wear on the ring.

1.2 Threading Prevention

Ensure current distribution is even. Uneven pressure leads to "threading" (grooves) on the slip ring surface, which requires expensive re-machining.

SOP 2: Dust & Moisture Control

The 1.3 MW units operate in a tropical zone. Moisture is the enemy of insulation.

2.1 Carbon Dust Removal

  • Method: Use Dry, Low-Pressure Compressed Air to blow dust out of rigging, followed by a specialized vacuum.
  • Solvents: Do NOT use solvents that leave a residue; residue attracts more dust.

2.2 Space Heaters

  • Rule: Whenever the unit is in STANDBY (Stopped), the generator space heaters MUST be ON.
  • Purpose: Keeps the windings above the dew point preventing condensation absorption.
SOP 3: Electrical Integrity Tests

You cannot see insulation failure until it explodes. You must test for it.

3.1 Insulation Resistance (IR / Megger)

  • Frequency: Annually.
  • Method:
    1. Isolate the generator (Open breakers).
    2. Apply 500V/1000V (depending on rating) DC to Stator and Rotor windings.
    3. Correction: Readings must be corrected to 40°C standard.

3.2 Terminal Box Heating

  • Inspection: Open the main terminal box annually.
  • Sign: Look for discolored lugs or melted insulation tape (Signs of high resistance heating).
  • Action: Clean and torque to spec.
Summary Checklist
  • Weekly: Inspect brushes for sparking (through viewing port).
  • Monthly: Clean carbon dust from rigging.
  • Annually: Megger Test & Terminal Box Torque check.
