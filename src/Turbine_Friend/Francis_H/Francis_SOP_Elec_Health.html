INVISIBLE DEGRADATION

Generator windings (6.6-11kV) degrade invisibly. Breakdown causes "Slot Discharge" and total winding failure. A stator rewind costs >$100k and months of downtime.

CORONA DETECTION

  • OZONE SMELL: Detect sharp, chlorine-like smell at air exits (Unit at >70% load). Indicates active discharge.
  • WHITE POWDER: Visual grey/white residue in winding slots is a byproduct of resin breakdown by ozone.

THERMAL BUDGET

Class Cont. Max Hot Spot
Class B 130°C 145°C
Class F 155°C 170°C

Typically, 1.3MW units use Class F. Nominal rise should be ~110°C.
