GRID SYNCHRONIZATION RISK

  • Never Adjust Droop While Synced: Changing settings under load causes immediate load shock/trip.
  • Island Mode Transition: Manual intervention REQUIRED before separating from Rwanda Grid. NO Auto-Switching.

DROOP SETTINGS (THE 4-6% RULE)

Formula: Droop (%) = (df / f_nom) x 100

Example (4%): at 48.0 Hz unit goes 100% Load. at 52.0 Hz unit goes 0% Load.

Operating Mode Setting Reason
Parallel (Grid) 4-6% Stable load sharing with national grid.
Parallel (Unit 1+2) 4-6% (SAME) Equal droop = equal load sharing.
Island Mode 0% (Isochronous) Single unit controls frequency exactly (Unit 2 OFF).

VORTEX ROUGH ZONES

Draft tube vortex causes frequency hunting (±0.1 Hz) even with perfect PID.

Load (MW) Vortex Status Governor Behavior
0 - 0.5 MW STRONG VORTEX Severe Hunting - AVOID
0.5 - 0.9 MW Moderate Acceptable with Air Injection
0.9 - 1.3 MW No Vortex Stable Operation
