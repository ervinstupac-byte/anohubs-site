Critical SOP: Instrumentation & Sensors

Sensory Logic & SCADA Reliability

⚠️ TRUST BUT VERIFY

Context: The SCADA system is the brain, but sensors are the eyes. If the eyes are blind, the brain makes fatal decisions.

  • The Risk: "Drifting" sensors can hide overheating bearings or falsely trip the unit.
  • Rule: Never trust a digital reading blindly without analog correlation (e.g., check the mercury thermometer!).
SOP 1: Temperature Validation (RTDs)

Resistance Temperature Detectors (RTDs) protect the Babbit bearings.

1.1 Calibration Protocol

  1. Reference: Use a certified calibrated thermometer (Ice bath 0°C or Boiling 100°C checks).
  2. Compare: Insert the reference probe into the bearing well (if available) or tape adjacent to the RTD.
  3. Tolerance: Variation > 1.0°C requires offsetting the SCADA signal or replacing the sensor definition.
SOP 2: Pressure Transducer Logic

Penstock and Spiral Case pressure data determine Net Head and hydraulic safety.

2.1 Zero & Span

  • Zeroing: With the Penstock drained (Atmospheric pressure), the sensor must read 0.00 Bar. If it reads 0.5 Bar, it has drifted.
  • Spanning: Compare against the mechanical Bourdon gauge (Analog) at full pressure.
  • Action: Adjust the 4-20mA scaling in the PLC if drift is linear; replace if erratic.
SOP 3: Flow Meter Hygiene

Silt is the enemy of ultrasonics and electromagnetics.

3.1 Sensor Cleaning

  • Type: Ultrasonic / Electromagnetic.
  • Problem: A layer of conductive silt or algae on the electrode faces interferes with the signal.
  • Protocol: Remove the sensor head annually. Wipe the sensing face gently with a soft cloth and alcohol. Do NOT scratch the electrode.
Summary Checklist
  • Monthly: Compare SCADA Temps vs. Analog Thermometers.
  • Annually: Zero-out Pressure Transducers (During dewatering).
  • Annually: Clean Flow Meter electrodes.
