⚙️ DISTRIBUTOR & GUIDE VANE SYNCHRONIZATION

The "Mechanical Geometry" of Flow Control

Preventing turbulent wake damage through precision alignment

⚠️ THE "SINGLE VANE" DISASTER

Mechanics of Failure:

  • If EVEN ONE guide vane is misaligned (e.g., Vane #4 is at 25° while others are at 20°):
  • It acts as a "flow spoiler," creating a massive, turbulent wake downstream.
  • This wake strikes the runner blades cyclically (13 times per revolution for a 13-blade runner).
  • Result: Localized cavitation pitting and eventual blade cracking in direct line with the bad vane.
Angle Verification & Regulating Ring Geometry

The Synchronization Protocol

The Regulating Ring moves all 20 guide vanes simultaneously. They must move in perfect unison.

Parameter Specification Method
Sync Tolerance ± 0.5 mm Opening difference between any two vanes
Vertical Seal Check 0.00 mm (Light Tight) Feeler Gauge / Light Test at 0%
Linkage Play < 0.10 mm Dial indicator on lever arm

Measurement Procedure:

  1. Lock Governor in "Manual Mode."
  2. Move Distributor to 50% Opening.
  3. Measure the "vent opening" (gap between trailing edge of Vane A and nose of Vane B) for all 20 vanes.
  4. Pass Criteria: If average is 80mm, no vane should be > 80.5mm or < 79.5mm.
  5. Adjust: Use the eccentric pin or turnbuckle on the linkage arm to correct outliers.
The "Light Test" - The Ultimate Seal Check

Verifying 0% Close (Metal-to-Metal Seal)

To prevent "Bypass Failure" (where MIV cannot open because pressure won't equalize), the distributor must seal tightly.

Execution Steps (Requires Dewatered Unit):

  1. Close Distributor: Command 0% Servo position. Apply "Squeeze Pressure" (if applicable).
  2. Enter Spiral Case: Personnel with strong LED floodlights enter the spiral case (Lock-out/Tag-out req!).
  3. Enter Runner: Second person enters the runner/draft tube area (darkness required).
  4. Shine Light: Shine floodlight on the vertical seal line of each vane from the spiral case side.
  5. Observe: Person in runner looks for light leaks.
    • No Light: Perfect seal.
    • Starry Points: Minor pitting (acceptable).
    • Visible Beam: Gap exists (> 0.2mm). MUST ADJUST.
Diagnostic Cross-Reference

Symptoms of Synchronization Failure

Symptom Likely Sync Issue
MIV Bypass won't Equalize Vanes not sealing at 0% (Light Test Failed)
Rhythmic "Chugging" Sound One vane loose (Shear pin broken)
High Vibration at specific load Vanes openings uneven (> 2mm diff) causing hydraulic imbalance

Shear Pin Note: If a shear pin breaks, that specific vane becomes "free-floating." It will flutter violently. Stop unit immediately to prevent it hitting the runner.
