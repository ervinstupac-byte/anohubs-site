Your Hydropower Plant as a Fortress: Taking Control of Maintenance

How to transform hydropower maintenance from reactive repairs to proactive discipline, focusing on owner knowledge and digital modernization.

The main challenge in our industry is not always machine failure, but the lack of owner knowledge that empowers service providers to prioritize profit over optimal, long-term solutions. Maintenance too often defaults to a reactive approach.

Overcoming Myths and Reclaiming Control

To overcome the cycle of expensive, reactive repairs, asset owners must realize that every component is critical. The current reactive model is financially unsustainable.

"Your hydropower plant can become a fortress resistant to systemic shocks. This is achieved by combining practical experience and modern innovation."

Keys to Independence

Here is how to transform your plant into a resilient fortress:

1. Precision as the Foundation

  • Non-Negotiable Tolerance: The foundation of machine longevity is assembly precision (0.05 mm/m).
  • Optimal, Not Maximal: Targeted repairs often outperform total replacements.

2. Digital Protocol and Modernization

  1. Digital Logbook: Centralized service history for accountability.
  2. Automatic Trash Rack Cleaners: Optimization of generation flow.
