The SCADA-to-CEO Gap: Translating Turbine Health into Financial Strategy

Analysis of the crucial disconnect between technical SCADA data and executive financial strategy. Introduction to the Hydro Health Index (HHI).

Traditional monitoring systems generate massive amounts of technical data but fail to answer the strategic questions asked by leadership. This is the crucial SCADA-to-CEO Gap.

1. The Problem: Data Overload

Plant operators receive thousands of data points daily, yet executives receive only simple efficiency reports. This disconnect means strategic decisions are made with incomplete risk assessments.

2. The Solution: The Hydro Health Index (HHI)

We solve the SCADA-to-CEO Gap by introducing the Hydro Health Index (HHI).

The HHI transforms raw SCADA data into an actionable financial metric, providing a clear, predictive measure of the asset's health.

HHI’s Impact on Strategic Leadership:

  1. Quantifiable Risk Mitigation: Leaders gain immediate insight into the true risk of the entire portfolio.
  2. Proactive Budgeting: The HHI predicts when wear and tear will occur.
