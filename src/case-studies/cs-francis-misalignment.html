01 The Threat Vector

The plant experienced frequent unplanned shutdowns and accelerating bearing wear (guide bearings lasting only 18 months vs. expected 5 years). Previous service providers attributed the issue to "normal component fatigue" and recommended standard replacements, ignoring the underlying geometry.

> RISK DETECTED: SYSTEMIC MISALIGNMENT (>0.15 mm/m)
> CONSEQUENCE: ACCELERATED BEARING DESTRUCTION

02 Tactical Execution
