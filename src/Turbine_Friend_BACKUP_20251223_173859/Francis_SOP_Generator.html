THE SILENT KILLER: CARBON DUST

Carbon brushes wear down, creating conductive dust. In Rwanda's humidity, this dust creates "tracking paths" for electricity to jump to the stator frame (Flash-over).

Cleanliness is not aesthetics; it is insulation integrity.

BRUSH PROTOCOL

  • REPLACE: At 25-30% length. Never wait for the wire shunt to touch the slip ring.
  • PRESSURE: Too soft = Sparking/Burning. Too hard = Rapid groove wear.

HEATER LOGIC

Whenever the unit is SHUT DOWN, anti-condensation heaters MUST be ACTIVE. This prevents windings from absorbing tropical moisture.

INTEGRITY TESTS

IR
INSULATION RESISTANCE (MEGGER):

Annual test. 500V/1000V DC on Stator/Rotor. Correct all readings to 40°C baseline.

BOX
TERMINAL BOX INSPECTION:

Check for discolored lugs or melted tape (high resistance). Tighten to torque spec.
