History & Evolution ↓

01 // THE LEGACY

Viktor Kaplan's 1913 Vision

In the early 20th century, Francis turbines failed at low heads and varying flows. Professor Viktor Kaplan developed a propeller with adjustable blades, mimicking an airplane wing underwater. It was the first "smart" turbine.

Today's Reality: Modern Kaplans are mechanical marvels. They maintain >92% efficiency from 30% to 100% load thanks to the "Cam Curve" synchronization. But this complexity introduces a fatal flaw: the Hub Mechanism.

03 // FAILURE MODES

Mechanical Complexity Failures

01

Hub Oil Leakage

The Kaplan hub is a pressure vessel filled with oil. A seal failure does two things: it releases oil into the river (Environmental Disaster) and allows water into the hub, destroying the precision servo mechanism.

PROTOCOL: Pressurized Seal Testing & Oil Analysis
02

Blade Trunnion Seizure

The bronze bushings on the blade roots operate under massive load with very little movement. If lubrication fails, they seize. A seized blade destroys the regulation capability, turning your smart Kaplan into a dumb Propeller.

PROTOCOL: Servo Pressure Monitoring
